Frequently asked questions

Does anyone need acting experience?

No. The session is not a theatre rehearsal and nobody has to perform. The exercises are about listening and reacting to the person in front of you, and anyone can do that.

Can you run it for a mixed team that doesn't all speak Latvian?

Yes. We run team building, games and whole evenings in English, and can mix English and Latvian if part of the team prefers it.

How long does a session take?

We recommend 2-3 hours in person and 60-90 minutes online. It can be the first half of the day, followed by an evening with our host.

What about introverts?

We start with games everyone plays at the same time, then move to pairs and small groups. Nobody is pulled on stage against their will, and watching and laughing from the side is fine too.

How much does it cost?

Improv team training starts from 350 €, an English-speaking host for the evening from 350 €, hidden roles from 250 € and a full show from 900 € (VAT not included). Send us the date and team size and we'll reply with a programme and a quote.
